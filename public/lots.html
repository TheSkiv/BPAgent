Vessels and their lots

Who owns each KIPIS lot. The owner is suggested from the stock reports (green border) — check it and press Confirm. Change it any time. # overrides the loading order (1 = first); left empty, trucks continue the lot already being loaded, else the oldest.

Find lots in KIPIS

Reads KIPIS for a cargo word (e.g. amonio, TSP, axan) and adds the lots in our storage above — useful when a vessel is delivered. KIPIS must be open.

Companies

Whose cargo each ordering company loads: its name in the Helm/Yara release lists (matched automatically where the names agree); or, with no release line of its own, the seller it buys from (like Transwina → Helm); and whether it also loads its own cargo (like Agrokoncernas). A release is used first, then Buys from, then own cargo.
