Who owns each lot

A truck loads only from its owner's lots. The owner is suggested from the stock reports (green outline) — check it and press Confirm. You can change it any time. # sets the loading order (1 = first); left empty, trucks finish the lot already being loaded, then the oldest.

Find lots in KIPIS

Type a cargo word (amonio, TSP, axan) to add the lots in our storage to the list above — handy when a vessel has just been discharged. KIPIS must be open.

Companies

Whose cargo each ordering company loads: its name in the Helm/Yara release lists (matched automatically when the names agree); or, if it has no release of its own, the seller it buys from (Transwina → Helm); and whether it also loads its own cargo (Agrokoncernas).
